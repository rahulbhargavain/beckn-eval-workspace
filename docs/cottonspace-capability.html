cottonspace.com: capability summary

Himalayan forest restoration and Kumaon field knowledge, operated by Mountain Precision Agriculture LLP, Bhimtal. Read from the public site on 2026-10-28.

The site presents one operating business line (forest-restoration pilots under India's Green Credit Programme) supported by field engineering for lakes and hill aquaculture, a smallholder value-chain design, and a large set of free reference atlases for the Kumaon and wider Himalayan region.

At a glance

EntityMountain Precision Agriculture LLP, Bhimtal (Kumaon, Uttarakhand)
Regulatory standingRegistered on the MoEFCC Green Credit Programme platform (administered by ICFRE). Entities bid for forest-land plots; planting starts only after a winning proposal is approved.
Minimum project sizeForest land allotted in parcels of 5 hectares or more
Outcome target40% canopy density by year five, assessed by drone photogrammetry and satellite baseline tracking; Green Credits issued after a five-year audit
Core methodAssisted Natural Regeneration (ANR): species matched to micro-climates, invasive-weed management, multi-tier functional canopies
GeographyKumaon hills (Nainital, Bhimtal, Naukuchiatal, Almora corridor); atlases extend from Kashmir to Arunachal

Important distinctions the site makes

Green Credits are not carbon credits. Credits for tree plantations under the Green Credit Programme are issued through ICFRE after a five-year audit. They are not credits under a carbon-market standard or India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme, and should not be assumed interchangeable.

Maturity labels. Forest Watch is a research preview; the cookbook is a draft for kitchen testing; the shed planner is illustrative; the hill-construction notes are not engineering sign-off.
